\paragraph{Seven-CNOT free-angle construction.}
Fine-step continuation with a fixed local gauge produced a word whose only
varying local angles are $\pi-2\theta$ and $2\pi-2\theta$. Rational-angle
reconstruction and full symbolic matrix multiplication verify the literal
identity for every real $\theta$, including the negative-reflection variant.
It uses seven CNOTs and eighteen physical $U_3$ gates, with no GP gate or
residual scalar. The complete time-ordered table is in the supplement;
the exact JSON word and regression checks are archived. The proved interior
bounds are now $4\le E_{\mathrm{CX}}\le7$ and
$3\le E_{\mathrm{arb}}\le5$. Five/seven minimality remains a conjecture.

\paragraph{Explicit obstruction scope.}
The supplement now names the alternating time-ordered edge words
$(ca,ab,ca,ab)$, $(ab,ca,ab,ca)$, $(cb,ab,cb,ab)$, and $(ab,cb,ab,cb)$,
under the condition that the two control-edge gates are individually
controlled on $c$ in its computational basis. The zero-branch normalization
then forces the prohibited doubled spectrum. The branch equation is written
out. No normalization of arbitrary control-edge gates is assumed, and
other four-gate layouts remain outside this partial argument.

\paragraph{Certificates and numerical fits.}
The supplement uses the two-SYC B certificate to explain why an unsuccessful
local-frame fit cannot disprove exact reachability. It does not claim that
we reproduced the referee's 64-restart experiment. Count certificates and
explicit shortest symbolic local frames remain separate deliverables.
