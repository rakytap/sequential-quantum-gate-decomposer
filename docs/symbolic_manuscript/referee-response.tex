\section*{1. Trace-$\pm2$ reflection family}
For $R_\theta=I-2|\psi_\theta\rangle\langle\psi_\theta|$, with
$|\psi_\theta\rangle=\cos\theta|00\rangle+\sin\theta|11\rangle$,
we give an explicit unitary $B_\theta$ satisfying
\[
 B_\theta R_\theta X_a B_\theta^\dagger=Z_aS_b.
\]
The five gates in time order are
\[
 \mathrm{CX}_{c,a},\quad B_{\theta,ab},\quad CS_{c,b},\quad
 CZ_{c,a},\quad B_{\theta,ab}^\dagger.
\]
The zero-control branch is identity and the one-control branch is
$B_\theta^\dagger Z_aS_bB_\theta X_a=R_\theta$.
The manuscript supplies the matrix and basis convention. Exact symbolic
multiplication verifies unitarity and the full literal identity with
$\theta$ free. The sign change adds a local control Z.
The proved interior bounds are consequently
\[
 4\le E_{\mathrm{CX}}\le8,\qquad
 3\le E_{\mathrm{arb}}\le5.
\]

We independently found a seven-CNOT layout with BQSKit and refined its
local gates at ten angle values, including $\pi/8$ and $0.3$. The archived
words use seven CXs and physical U3 locals, with no GP gate. Independent
Qiskit matrix replays have maximum literal entry error below $10^{-12}$.
These are numerical witnesses, not a free-angle upper-bound proof.
The seeds, layout, complete words, versions, replay report, and reproduction
harness are included.

We also prove a partial doubled-spectrum obstruction for
$R_\theta(A\otimes I)$, for any one-qubit unitary $A$; the supplement gives
the determinant argument and its limited layout scope. We have not extended
Yu--Ying's exclusion to every four-gate layout. Their Fredkin reductions
use literal wire-SWAP identities that do not automatically apply to the
interior reflections. The main paper therefore states the conjecture
$E_{\mathrm{arb}}=5$ and $E_{\mathrm{CX}}=7$ for $0<\theta\le\pi/4$
explicitly, without claiming that numerical failures prove a lower bound.

\section*{2. Monodromy-polytope certificates for native columns}
We implemented exact count certificates from the corrected
Peterson--Crooks--Smith inequalities. The quantum Littlewood--Richardson
data retain their authors' source revision, hash, attribution, and license.
The implementation includes both projective-alcove branches at every
composition. Numerical eigenangles and linear programming propose candidates
only: exact complete Makhlin invariants accept target coordinates, rational
primal points prove reachability, and rational Farkas multipliers exclude
all branches at smaller depths.

The original numerical audit contained 150 target/native pairs in the SYC
and square-root-SWAP columns. The first exact pass proves 111 minimum
counts and one lower bound. A deeper query resolves that last record:
SYC needs exactly five square-root-SWAP pulses with arbitrary local gates;
the displayed atlas word still uses six. All 64 fixed-target/native pairs
in this subset now have exact minimum-count certificates. The other proved
records concern named parameter substitutions. Thirty proof deadlines and
eight uncertified coordinate conversions remain explicitly inconclusive.

Examples include three square-root-SWAPs for CS and B, four for CT and
iSWAP, and two SYCs for B. Thus the audit also certifies that three
square-root-SWAPs are not universal. An independent rational checker
reconstructs every accepted feasibility and exclusion certificate.

These results prove counts with arbitrary local $U(2)$ frames. The supplied
U3 and PhasedXZ alphabets generate those frames without a GP gate through
the checked phase-absorption identities. They do not give explicit shortest
symbolic frame words or local-count minima. Atlas panels and the CSV now
distinguish proved fixed-target minima, proved counts at one family sample,
and remaining numerical leads. A hard sampled member plus a matching
full-family construction can separately certify a minimum uniform family
budget, which is labelled as such. Displayed long constructions retain
their visible gaps; no circuit is silently replaced by an existence proof.
